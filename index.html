Data sources

QLD: Department of Resources DCDB cadastre; Vegetation management (regulated vegetation, essential habitat, wetlands, watercourses, PMAV); Regional ecosystem mapping; Land types of Queensland; Queensland land use (QLUMP); Floodplain assessment overlay; historic flood lines. NSW: Spatial Services NSW cadastre; State Vegetation Type Map (extant plant community types); Land and Soil Capability v4 (DCCEEW); NSW Landuse 2017; EPI flood planning areas (NSW Planning). Information is indicative only; check official sources (QLD Globe, SIX Maps, vegetation management reports) before relying on it.
